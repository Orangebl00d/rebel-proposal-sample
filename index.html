04Large and unexpected

Rebel and Sedera.

Rebel is your everyday and preventive care. Sedera is for the large and unexpected: an accident, a surgery, a hospital stay.

Sedera.

A voluntary medical cost sharing community, not insurance. Members share each other's eligible large medical expenses. Rebel Health Alliance members use it, John included.

A medical need.

You pay the Initial Unshareable Amount (IUA) first. Sedera's team negotiates the bill. The community shares eligible costs above that amount. At most three IUAs per household in a year.

Existing conditions.

Sedera looks back 36 months. Costs tied to a condition from that window are not shared in year one, are shared up to $25,000 in year two and $50,000 in year three, and are fully shareable from year four. No one is turned away for one.

Prescriptions.

A prescription marketplace with low cash prices on most common medications.
